Reference images for identification (spec 7.5). One clear side-on photo per catalogue model, named after
`referenceImg` in `data/catalogue.json` (e.g. `nike-pegasus-40.jpg`). JPEG, PNG or WebP.
Use images you have the rights to (e.g. your own photos). Missing files are skipped: identification
then relies on the candidate names and the listing photos only.
